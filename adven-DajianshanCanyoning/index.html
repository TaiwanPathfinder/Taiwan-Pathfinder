Dajianshan Waterfall Canyoning

A Rappelling Adventure Down Multiple Waterfalls

An outdoor adventure that combines exploration with an introduction to technique: you'll use rope skills to make your way down a series of waterfalls and slippery terrain. It's about more than the thrill — with professional guidance, you'll build safety awareness, movement skills, and the confidence and judgment to handle a canyon environment.

Activity Introduction

Duration

About 5–6 hours

Difficulty

A rappelling adventure: with a professional guide, you'll learn basic rope skills and descend waterfall by waterfall through slippery canyon terrain. It's beginner level, but you should be comfortable with heights and have the basic fitness to walk on wet, uneven ground.

Highlights

Descend multiple waterfalls (about 5–30 m) in a single trip

Learn basic canyoning and rope skills: putting on your gear, handling the rope, rappelling posture, and moving safely on slippery rock

Build confidence moving vertically and in wet terrain

Professional guides keep you safe and coach your technique throughout

Small groups for a better and safer experience

Things to Know

Wear long-sleeved quick-dry clothing and avoid cotton; bring a change of clothes and a towel.

Bring at least 1–1.5 liters of water and light food or snacks, packed in a dry bag.

Wetsuits are provided in winter or cold weather.

If you have long hair, bring a hair tie.

You should be comfortable with heights and have basic fitness to walk on slippery, uneven terrain and descend waterfalls.

Technical gear (helmet, harness, ropes, canyoning shoes, descender) is provided.

Tell us about any health conditions or special needs when you register.

A full pre-trip briefing, including weather advice and final reminders, is sent three days before departure.

Weather-Related Cancellations or Rescheduling

Force majeure: If the trip cannot proceed due to a land typhoon warning, earthquake, cold wave, heavy rain advisory (as defined by Taiwan's Central Weather Administration), road closures, or restrictions from authorities, you'll be refunded minus a 5% administrative fee.

Poor forecasts: For conditions like persistent rain or a cold front that could affect the experience, we'll make a call and announce it no later than 3 days before departure. If we cancel, you'll be refunded minus a 5% administrative fee.

Water levels and stream conditions are assessed by your guide before the trip.

Cancellations for Personal Reasons

14+ days before departure: Refund minus a 5% administrative fee.

8–13 days before departure: Refund minus 20% of necessary costs.

3–7 days before departure: Refund minus 30% of necessary costs.

Within 2 days of departure: No refund.

Minimum Group Size

If the trip is canceled due to not meeting the minimum group size, you can choose a free reschedule or a refund minus a 5% administrative fee.
