Traditional Climbing Workshop

Learn to Protect Yourself on Natural Rock

Most climbing outings clip you into bolts that are already there. Trad climbing is different — you read the rock, choose where to place your own protection, and decide how to move. This hands-on workshop shows you how it works, with the gear in your hands.

Activity Introduction

Duration

1 day, about 6 hours

Difficulty

Beginner — climbers who already know lead belaying are preferred

An experiential workshop to help you understand the spirit of trad climbing, handle the gear yourself, and learn what to prepare as a beginner and how to maintain it.

What Makes This Different

Typical climbing outing

  • Follow fixed bolts
  • Focus on movement and grade
  • Gear is placed for you

This workshop

  • Choose and place your own protection
  • Focus on reading rock, gear and risk
  • You handle every piece of gear and learn why it holds

A different way of climbing — slower, more thoughtful, and a lot more self-reliant.

Highlights

Trad vs. Sport ClimbingUnderstand the spirit and differences of both styles, and experience the charm of trad climbing.

Complete Gear IntroductionCams, nuts, alpine quickdraws, tips on choosing trad gear, and maintenance know-how.

Protection Placement PracticeLearn how to choose cracks, place gear and check it, in a safe environment.

Mock Lead ClimbingSafely simulate the feeling of placing protection while leading.

Who Can Join

  • Curious about trad climbing techniques
  • Climbers who want to take a trad course and are preparing to invest in related gear
  • Anyone who wants to experience the freedom of climbing on natural rock

Weather-Related Cancellations or Rescheduling

Force majeure: If the trip cannot proceed due to a land typhoon warning, earthquake, cold wave, heavy rain advisory (as defined by Taiwan's Central Weather Administration), road closures, or restrictions from authorities, you'll be refunded minus a 5% administrative fee.

Poor forecasts: For conditions like persistent rain or a cold front that could affect the experience, we'll make a call and announce it no later than 3 days before departure. If we cancel, you'll be refunded minus a 5% administrative fee.

Cancellations for Personal Reasons

8–13 days before departure: Refund minus 20% of necessary costs.

3–7 days before departure: Refund minus 30% of necessary costs.
