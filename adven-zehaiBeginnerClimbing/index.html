Rehai Beginner Rock Climbing

Rehai Crag is located in Beitou District, Taipei City, near the famous Beitou hot springs and the Datun mountain range — one of the few natural climbing spots within the city. No experience needed: the routes here are designed for beginners, and your guide will be with you every step of the way as you complete your first outdoor climb.

A one-day climbing experience — pure outdoor power.

Step out of the climbing gym and onto real rock! Rehai Crag is easy to reach from Beitou, yet feels a world away from the city's bustle. Known for its solid andesite walls with excellent friction, no experience or gear of your own is required — your guide will start from the absolute basics, so you can safely experience your first outdoor climb. The trip is led by a professional guide, walking you step by step through gear briefing, basic safety concepts, and belay technique before you actually climb, letting you feel the appeal of outdoor climbing for yourself. Between climbs, you can also take in views of Mt. Datun and the hills around Beitou.

Highlights

Things to Know

Wear comfortable, breathable outdoor clothing, and bring a windproof or rain jacket in case of changing weather.

Bring around 2 liters of water, lunch, and sun protection (sunscreen, hat, sunglasses, etc.).

If you own climbing shoes, feel free to bring them for a better fit — we also provide climbing shoes, though sizing may not be a perfect match.

All necessary climbing gear (harness, helmet, etc.) and activity insurance are provided, so you can enjoy the day worry-free.

Please let us know about any special needs or health conditions when you register, so we can plan accordingly.

We'll send a full pre-trip briefing three days before departure, including weather advice and last-minute reminders.

Weather-Related Cancellations or Rescheduling

Force majeure: If the trip cannot proceed due to a land typhoon warning, earthquake, cold wave, heavy rain advisory (as defined by Taiwan's Central Weather Administration), road closures, or restrictions from authorities, you'll be refunded minus a 5% administrative fee.

Poor forecasts: For conditions like persistent rain or a cold front that could affect the experience, we'll make a call and announce it no later than 3 days before departure. If we cancel, you'll be refunded minus a 5% administrative fee.

Cancellations for Personal Reasons

14+ days before departure: Refund minus a 5% administrative fee.

8–13 days before departure: Refund minus 20% of necessary costs.

3–7 days before departure: Refund minus 30% of necessary costs.

Within 2 days of departure: No refund.

Minimum Group Size

If the trip is canceled due to not meeting the minimum group size, you can choose a free reschedule or a refund minus a 5% administrative fee.
