Who We Are

We are Taiwan Pathfinder — a team of outdoor guides and mountaineers born and raised right here in Taiwan. Driven by our love for the outdoors, we're on a mission to share Taiwan's breathtaking mountains, trails, and untouched wilderness with the world.

We know how often language barriers keep visitors from truly experiencing Taiwan's natural beauty — that's why we're here: to make every adventure safe, accessible, and meaningful.

Backed by professional certifications and real-world experience, we guide, support, and inspire every person who joins us. Let us walk beside you as you discover Taiwan's mountains, one trail at a time, on a journey you won't forget.
