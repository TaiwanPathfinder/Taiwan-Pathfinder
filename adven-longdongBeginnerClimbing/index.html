Things to Know

Wear comfortable, breathable outdoor clothing, and bring a windproof or rain jacket.

Bring around 2 liters of water, lunch, and sun protection.

If you own climbing shoes, feel free to bring them for a better fit.

All necessary climbing gear and activity insurance are provided.

Weather-Related Cancellations or Rescheduling

Force majeure: If the trip cannot proceed due to a land typhoon warning, earthquake, cold wave, heavy rain advisory (as defined by Taiwan's Central Weather Administration), road closures, or restrictions from authorities, you'll be refunded minus a 5% administrative fee.

Poor forecasts: For conditions like persistent rain or a cold front that could affect the experience, we'll make a call and announce it no later than 3 days before departure. If we cancel, you'll be refunded minus a 5% administrative fee.

Cancellations for Personal Reasons

14+ days before departure: Refund minus a 5% administrative fee.

8–13 days before departure: Refund minus 20% of necessary costs.

3–7 days before departure: Refund minus 30% of necessary costs.

Within 2 days of departure: No refund.

Minimum Group Size

If the trip is canceled due to not meeting the minimum group size, you can choose a free reschedule or a refund minus a 5% administrative fee.
