Our Story

Taiwan Pathfinder was founded in 2024 by a group of outdoor professionals who were born and raised in Taiwan. We started guiding because we wanted to share the places we love — Taiwan's mountains, sea cliffs and trails — with people from around the world.

We noticed how often language and unfamiliarity keep visitors from the best of Taiwan's outdoors. So we built our trips to be clear, welcoming and well prepared, guided in English or Mandarin from start to finish.

What We Stand For

Local RootsWe're guides who live here, with real ties to Taiwan's land, culture and communities.

Bilingual GuidingEvery hike, climb and expedition can be guided in English or Mandarin, so you can focus on the experience.

Safety FirstSmall groups, trained guides and proper insurance on every trip.

More Than a TripAlong the trail, you'll learn about the history, culture and traditions behind the landscape.

How We Keep You Safe

  • Guide-to-participant ratio of no more than 1:6
  • Every participant is insured: NT$2,000,000 accidental death and disability, NT$200,000 medical expenses
  • Guides trained in first aid and incident management
  • A detailed pre-trip briefing sent three days before departure
