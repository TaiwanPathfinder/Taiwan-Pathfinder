Longdong Deep Water Solo (DWS)

No climbing experience? Can't swim? As long as you're comfortable being in the water, you can still join.

Deep water soloing looks like rock climbing, but it's really just climbing without ropes above deep water — when you slip, you simply drop safely into the sea. We start with easy shoreline traverses to warm up and teach proper water-entry and falling technique.

For those who want more, we gradually work up to the classic route "The Blue Temptation." Your guide keeps watch from the shore and follows alongside in the water with flotation support throughout, and life vests are provided for anyone who can't swim.

Activity Introduction

Duration

4–5 hours (excluding transportation)

Difficulty

Beginner Friendly

Enjoy the freedom of deep water soloing along the Longdong coast. The session begins with a shoreline safety briefing covering tides, sea conditions, and proper water entry techniques. We then warm up with low-level traversing before gradually moving on to higher problems above deep water. Those with sufficient ability may challenge the classic route "The Blue Temptation."

No climbing experience is required, and you don't need to know how to swim — as long as you're comfortable being in the water. Your guide provides supervision and safety support from the shore and follows alongside in the water, selects appropriate areas based on tidal conditions, and demonstrates safe exits and re-entry to the rock. Life vests are provided as needed.

Things to Know

Please wear comfortable, breathable outdoor clothing and bring a windproof or waterproof jacket in case of changing coastal weather.

Prepare around 2 liters of water, lunch, and sun protection (sunscreen, hat, sunglasses, etc.).

If you have your own climbing shoes, we recommend bringing them for a better fit; climbing shoes are also provided, though sizes may not be perfect.

All essential climbing equipment (harness, etc.) and activity insurance will be provided, so you can enjoy the activity worry-free.

If you have any special needs or health conditions, please inform us during registration so we can make appropriate arrangements.

A detailed trip briefing, including weather suggestions and final reminders, will be sent 3 days before departure to help you prepare.

Trip Cancellation or Rescheduling Due to Weather

Force Majeure: If the trip cannot proceed due to events such as a land typhoon warning, earthquake, cold wave, heavy rain advisory (as defined by the Central Weather Administration), road closures, or restrictions imposed by management authorities, a refund will be issued after deducting a 5% administrative fee.

Poor Weather Forecast: If adverse weather such as continuous rain or an incoming cold front may affect the trip quality, we will conduct a comprehensive assessment. The final decision will be announced no later than 3 days before departure. If we decide to cancel the trip, a refund will be issued after deducting a 5% administrative fee.

Cancellation Due to Personal Reasons

Cancel 14 days before departure: Refund minus a 5% administrative fee.

Cancel 8–13 days before departure: 20% of the booking fee will be deducted.

Cancel 3–7 days before departure: 30% of the booking fee will be deducted.

Cancel within 2 days of departure: No refund.

Minimum Participant Requirement

If the minimum participant number is not reached and the trip is canceled, participants may choose to reschedule for free or receive a refund with a 5% administrative fee deducted.
