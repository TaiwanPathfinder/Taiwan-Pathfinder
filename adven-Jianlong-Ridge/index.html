Activity Introduction

Duration

Jianlong Ridge takes its name from its jagged profile, resembling a stegosaurus's spine — it's an intermediate-level route that requires scrambling with both hands and feet across exposed rock ridges.

Highlights

Forest scenery gives way to exposed rock ridges along the route, with sweeping views — a favorite for hikers who love that "ridge-walking" feeling

Some sections require scrambling with both hands and feet across steep rock ridges, for a more thrilling experience than your average trail

Led by professional guides from start to finish

Best suited to those with some hiking experience who want to take on a more challenging route

We recommend having some basic hiking experience before joining, since parts of the route require scrambling with hands and feet across rock ridges. If you have no outdoor experience at all, we'd suggest starting with one of our beginner-friendly routes first.

The hike is about 5–6 hours of walking in total. The pace isn't rushed, but we recommend having a regular exercise habit so you can handle a longer day on the trail.

Since the route includes scrambling across rock ridges, we don't recommend it for young children or participants with limited mobility. If you're unsure, feel free to contact us before registering.

We'll assess the weather conditions and make the call. If we cancel due to weather, you can transfer to another session or get a refund (minus a 5% handling fee).

Things to Know

The pace is relaxed, but participants should be able to walk 5–6 hours on mountain trails.

Please wear appropriate hiking shoes and bring rain gear, sun protection, and drinking water.

In case of bad weather, the trip may be postponed or canceled. You may transfer to another Taiwan Pathfinder trip (5% handling fee applies for refunds).

Trip Cancellation or Rescheduling Due to Weather

Force Majeure: If the trip cannot proceed due to events such as a land typhoon warning, earthquake, cold wave, heavy rain advisory (as defined by the Central Weather Administration), road closures, or restrictions imposed by management authorities, a refund will be issued after deducting a 5% administrative fee.

Poor Weather Forecast: If adverse weather such as continuous rain or an incoming cold front may affect the trip quality, we will conduct a comprehensive assessment. The final decision will be announced no later than 3 days before departure. If we decide to cancel the trip, a refund will be issued after deducting a 5% administrative fee.

Cancellation Due to Personal Reasons

Cancel 14 days before departure: Refund minus a 5% administrative fee.

Cancel 8–13 days before departure: 20% of the booking fee will be deducted.

Cancel 3–7 days before departure: 30% of the booking fee will be deducted.

Cancel within 2 days of departure: No refund.

Minimum Participant Requirement

If the minimum participant number is not reached and the trip is canceled, participants may choose to reschedule for free or receive a refund with a 5% administrative fee deducted.
