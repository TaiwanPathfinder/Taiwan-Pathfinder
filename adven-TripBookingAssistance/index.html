Mountain Permit & Booking Assistance Service

To help international travelers arrange hiking trips in Taiwan more smoothly, we provide the following application and booking assistance services.

Fee

From NTD 550

FILL BOOKING FORM
1

Mountain Permit Application Assistance

Fee: NTD 550 per person

Service includes:

  • National Park Permit application
  • Mountain Entry Permit application
  • Administrative assistance for the hiking trip

Please note:

  • This fee covers administrative processing and is non-refundable, regardless of whether the permit or lottery application is successful.
  • Foreign hikers usually have access to reserved quota spaces. We strongly recommend applying at least 4 months in advance to increase the chance of securing a spot.
2

Shuttle & Mountain Hut Meal Booking Assistance

If you require assistance with:

  • Transportation / shuttle arrangements
  • Mountain hut meals
  • Other related bookings

We charge an additional 10% administrative booking fee based on the total booking amount.

A full quotation breakdown will be provided.

3

Guide & Porter Services

If you require:

  • Hiking guides
  • Porter services
  • Customized trekking arrangements

Please contact us directly by email, and we will provide a quotation based on your itinerary.
